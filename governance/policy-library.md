# Policy Library

## Purpose

The Policy Library defines the governance documents that establish NimbusVault's security and compliance program. These policies provide the foundation for security controls, assign accountability, and support compliance with industry frameworks. All policies are reviewed at least annually and approved by executive leadership.

| **Policy Document** | **Purpose** | **Policy Owner** | **Review Frequency** | **Last Approved** | **Framework Alignment** | **Status** |
