## Asset Inventory (Critical Assets)

Maintaining an accurate inventory of critical information assets is foundational to effective Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC). This inventory establishes ownership, data classification, and business criticality to support risk assessments, control implementation, incident response, and audit readiness.
