NimbusVault Cloud recently underwent a comprehensive Audit Readiness and Compliance gap assessment to prepare for our upcoming multi-framework audits (SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI DSS, and FedRAMP Ready).
The goal of this initiative was to evaluate the design and operating effectiveness of our internal controls, identify risks within our Azure-hosted environment, and ensure our security posture aligns with the strict regulatory requirements of our Healthcare, Finance, and Government clients.
Overall, NimbusVault demonstrates a mature security baseline, particularly in data encryption and endpoint protection. However, the mock audit identified key areas requiring remediation prior to external attestation, specifically regarding the consistency of user access reviews and vendor risk management documentation. Addressing these findings will not only secure our compliance goals but accelerate enterprise sales cycles by demonstrating a verifiable commitment to cyber resilience.
